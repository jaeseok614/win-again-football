# English football pyramid references (2026/27)

The career mode follows the five English men's levels from the Premier League down to the National League. The game uses eight teams per division, so these are representative clubs rather than the complete 20- or 24-club league memberships. Display names remain fictional parodies; the reference names below are development metadata and are not shown in the game. Club IDs and ability ratings stay stable for saved campaigns.

The story keeps Tottunham as the manager's starting club in division five, even though Tottenham Hotspur is a Premier League reference. The seven opponents in that opening division are current National League clubs. Promotion then moves the campaign through League Two, League One, the Championship and the Premier League.

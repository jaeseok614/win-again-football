# English football pyramid references (2026/27)

The career mode follows the five English men's levels from the Premier League down to the National League. The current compact game format uses eight teams per division, so these are representative clubs rather than the complete 20- or 24-club league memberships. Display names remain fictional parodies; the match preparation report now identifies each opponent's real-club inspiration. Club IDs and ability ratings stay stable for saved campaigns.

The story keeps Tottunham as the manager's starting club in division five, even though Tottenham Hotspur is a Premier League reference. The seven opponents in that opening division are current National League clubs. Promotion then moves the campaign through League Two, League One, the Championship and the Premier League.

## Competition size request (2026-10-06)

The owner wants future new careers to use the full English division sizes: 20
Premier League clubs and 24 in each division below, giving 38 or 46 home/away
matches respectively. Current saves and the current compact game still use eight
clubs and 14 rounds. Treat the full-size pool as unfinished until fixture,
finance, staffing, statistics, history validation and mobile UI all support the
variable season length together.

The match report shows the referenced real club alongside its fictional display
name. Its 1–99 player overall is calculated from the game's fictional abilities
with position-specific weights. FotMob ratings are a scouting/benchmarking
reference, not copied player ratings or a live data feed.

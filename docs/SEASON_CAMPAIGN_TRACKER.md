# Live season campaign tracker

The manager's office shows the active five-tier season objective beside the
career milestones. Divisions 2–5 track the top-two promotion places and the
points gap to the promotion line; division 1 tracks the top-six board target.
The tracker also flags relegation when the club is in seventh or eighth place
in a division that has a lower neighbor. It changes to a final outcome after
the league schedule is complete.

The objective is a view over confirmed standings and the active fixture count.
It adds no campaign fields, rewards, hidden matches, RNG draws or automatic
season transitions. Historical saves keep their own fixture totals, and the
season progress gauge uses that exact total (38, 46 or the legacy schedule).
The panel renders only with the manager's office, so it adds no work to live
match ticks.

The presentation takes a broad cue from EA SPORTS FC 26 Career Mode's
objective-based Manager Live Challenges, then adapts the idea to the game's
existing promotion pyramid and board goals. It does not reproduce challenge
scenarios or player data. See [EA SPORTS FC 26 Career Mode](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/features/fc-26-career-mode)
and [the five-division schedule and movement rules](FIVE_TIER_PYRAMID_AND_FINANCE.md).
